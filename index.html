JP Art Days Calendar

This is a site to list various days that the Japanese art community will post art related to specific themes.

They primarily use Number Substitution (語呂合わせ) for these themes. Some are based around Holidays or other commemorative dates.

I am not an expert in Japanese puns, so my interpretations may be off, feel free to reach out if you have corrections or suggestions!

A lot of dates are in November because of the pun "いい" (11) which means "Nice, good, etc, so we end up with a lot of Nice ___ Days".
